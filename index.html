About This Project

The Stablecoin Regulatory Radar tracks regulatory developments affecting stablecoins and digital payments across four key jurisdictions. Each day, an automated pipeline fetches regulatory news from RSS feeds, analyzes it using AI (DeepSeek), and updates this dashboard with structured summaries and impact assessments.

Methodology

  1. Collect — RSS feeds from Google News, CoinDesk, CoinTelegraph, and The Block are fetched daily for jurisdiction-specific keywords.
  2. Analyze — Each article is processed by an LLM (DeepSeek) that summarizes the regulatory development, classifies its impact, and scores the regulatory climate for that jurisdiction (1 = restrictive, 10 = innovation-friendly).
  3. Generate — Analysis results are aggregated into jurisdiction scores and a chronological timeline, then deployed to this static site.

Monitored Jurisdictions

🇭🇰 Hong Kong — HKMA (Stored Value Facility + upcoming stablecoin licensing)
🇺🇸 United States — Fed / OCC / SEC (federal stablecoin legislation + state-level frameworks)
🇪🇺 European Union — EBA / ESMA (MiCA regulation)
🇸🇬 Singapore — MAS (Payment Services Act + stablecoin framework)
